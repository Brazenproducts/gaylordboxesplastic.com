Plastic Gaylord Boxes: Load Ratings and Stacking Specs

Gaylordboxesplastic Editorial • October 2026

Load rating is the single most misread spec on a plastic gaylord box. Buyers see one capacity figure on a datasheet and assume it covers every situation, when in practice a bulk bin carries three different load numbers depending on how it is used. Specifying to the wrong one causes early failure, dropped loads, and rack-safety problems. This reference explains the ratings that matter and how to size a gaylord to your actual warehouse.

The Three Load Numbers

Every reusable bulk container should be quoted against three distinct capacities. Confusing them is the most common sizing error in bulk-handling procurement.

  • Static load: the maximum weight the box can hold while sitting still on a flat, supported surface.
  • Dynamic load: the weight it can safely carry while being lifted and moved by forklift or pallet jack — always lower than static.
  • Rack load: the weight it can bear when supported only at its edges on beam racking, which stresses the base very differently from floor support.

Why the Numbers Diverge

A floor-supported box transfers load straight down through a continuous surface. A racked box must span the gap between beams, putting its base and lower walls into bending. A box with a 1,000 kg static rating might carry only 600 kg racked. If your operation stores gaylords on beam racking, the rack figure — not the static figure — is the number that governs your purchase. Stacking and Column Strength

When gaylords stack directly on one another, the bottom box carries the full weight of everything above it plus its own contents. Design the stack so the base unit's static rating covers the total column, then apply a safety factor for handling and humidity. For most B2B programs we recommend sizing the bottom box to at least 1.5x the calculated column weight. Also confirm the lid or top frame transfers load through the walls, not the contents, so stacking does not crush the product inside.

Matching Bin to Pallet and Flow

Load rating works together with footprint. A gaylord sized to a standard 48x40 pallet distributes weight predictably and integrates with existing material handling. Pair the bin with a pallet rated for the same dynamic load — an underrated pallet becomes the weak link under a fully loaded box. Elipacko offers compatible plastic pallets matched to its gaylord line so the bin and base share a consistent load rating rather than leaving a mismatch in the stack.

Environmental Derating

Polypropylene softens slightly at elevated temperature and stiffens in cold. If your bins live in a hot warehouse or a freezer, ask for the rating at your actual operating temperature, not just at 23 °C lab conditions. Sustained load at high temperature also causes creep — slow deformation over time — so long-dwell storage needs a more conservative figure than short-cycle handling.

Spec Checklist

  • Identify whether bins are floor-stored, stacked, or racked.
  • Use the rack rating if beam racking is involved.
  • Size the base box to 1.5x the stacked column weight.
  • Match pallet dynamic rating to the loaded bin.
  • Request ratings at your real operating temperature.
